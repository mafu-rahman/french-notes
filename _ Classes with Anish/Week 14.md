## Feminine of nouns and adjectives

### Rule 1 - add 'e' to the masculine form
- e.g., ami - amie
- petit - petite
- Sûr - Sûre
- lourd - lourde

### Rule 2 - words ending in 'e' do not change.
- facile - facile (easy)
- Le concierge - (caretaker)

### Rule 3 - words ending in 'er' - change to 'ère'
- l'étranger - l'étrangère
- Cher - chère

### Rule 4 - words ending in 'f' - change to 've'
- Vif - vive
- actif - active
- sportif - sportive
- neuf - neuve

### Rule 5 - words ending in 'x' change to 'se'
- heureux - heureuse
- ambitieux - ambitieuse
- Dangereux - Dangereuse

> **exceptions**
> - doux - douce
> - faux - fausse
> - roux - rousse
> - vieux - vieille


### Rule 6 - adjectives in 'c', change to 'che' or 'que'
- blanc - blanche
- sec - sèche

- turc - turque
- grec - grecque


### Rule 7 - words ending in 'el', 'eil', 'en', 'on', 'et', 'ot', 'as', 'os' -> we ==double the final consonant and add 'e'==
- =='el', 'eil', 'en', 'on', 'et', 'ot', 'as', 'et', 'os'==


|     | masculine | feminine   |
| --- | --------- | ---------- |
| el  | cruel     | cruelle    |

| et  | net       | nette      |
| ot  | sot       | sotte      |
| as  | bas       | basse      |
| os  | gros      | grosse     |
