## 🔊 Vowel Sounds (Basic)

These are the "pure" vowel sounds, not the nasal ones.

| Vowel | Example | Pronunciation |
|-------|---------|---|
| **a** | chat (cat) | AH (open mouth) |
| **e** | été (summer) | AY (smile, raise cheeks) |
| **i** | oui (yes) | EE (teeth together, smile) |
| **o** | eau (water) | OH (lips rounded) |
| **u** | tu (you) | OO (lips very rounded forward) |
| **y** | y (letter Y) | EE (same as i) |

## 🗣️ Consonant Sounds

Most consonants sound similar to English, with these exceptions:

| Letter | Sound | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **C + a,o,u** | K | cat = **KAH** |
| **C + e,i** | S | cerise = say-**REEZ** |
| **Ç (c cedilla)** | S | ça = **SAH** |
| **G + a,o,u** | G | gare = **GAR** |
| **G + e,i** | ZH | gens = **ZHHOHN** |
| **J** | ZH | je = **ZHUH** |
| **H** | Silent | hôtel = oh-**TEL** |
| **R** | Throat guttural | rare = **RAR** (from throat) |
| **W** | V | wagon = **vah-GOHN** |
| **S** | Z (between vowels) | rose = **ROHZ** |
| **S** | S (start of word) | salle = **SAL** |
| **Z** | Z | zèbre = **ZEB-ruh** |

### Special Note: The French R
The French **r** is made in the back of your throat, not with your tongue.
- **English R**: Make it with your tongue
- **French R**: Make it with a guttural sound from your throat, like you're gargling

## 🎵 The Schwa (ə)

The neutral vowel **ə** (like the 'a' in "about") is common in French. It's unstressed and quiet.

- **le** = luh
- **de** = duh
- **ce** = suh

It's often dropped in speech, so "je" sounds like "zh" not "zhuh".
