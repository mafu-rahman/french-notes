# 🗣️ The Imperative Mood (Commands)

The **imperative** is used to give orders, instructions, or suggestions. It uses three forms: **tu**, **nous**, and **vous** — but **without the subject pronoun**.

---

## 📋 Formation: Regular Verbs

Take the **present tense** forms of **tu / nous / vous** and drop the subject pronoun.

### -ER Verbs (e.g., parler — to speak)

| Form | Present | Imperative | Meaning |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| tu | tu parles | **Parle !** | Speak! |
| nous | nous parlons | **Parlons !** | Let's speak! |
| vous | vous parlez | **Parlez !** | Speak! (formal/plural) |

> ⚠️ **Special rule for -ER verbs (tu form):** Drop the **-s** from the *tu* form.
> tu parles → **Parle** (not "parles")

### -IR Verbs (e.g., finir — to finish)

| Form | Present | Imperative | Meaning |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| tu | tu finis | **Finis !** | Finish! |
| nous | nous finissons | **Finissons !** | Let's finish! |
| vous | vous finissez | **Finissez !** | Finish! (formal/plural) |

> ✅ For -IR verbs, the *tu* form **keeps its -s** (unlike -ER verbs).

---

## ⚡ Irregular Imperative Forms

Some verbs have **special imperative forms** that don't follow the regular rules. ==Memorise these!==

| Verb                  | tu          | nous          | vous         |
| --------------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| **être** (to be)      | **sois**    | **soyons**    | **soyez**    |
| **avoir** (to have)   | **aie**     | **ayons**     | **ayez**     |
| **savoir** (to know)  | **sache**   | **sachons**   | **sachez**   |
| **vouloir** (to want) | **veuille** | *(veuillons)* | **veuillez** |
| **aller** (to go)     | **va**      | **allons**    | **allez**    |

### Examples

- **Sois** sage ! → Be good! *(to a child)*
- **Soyez** patient ! → Be patient! *(formal/plural)*
- **Aie** confiance ! → Have confidence!
- **Ayons** de la patience. → Let's have patience.
- **Sache** écouter. → Know how to listen.
- **Veuillez** patienter. → Please wait. *(very polite/formal — used in official settings)*
- **Va** au marché ! → Go to the market!
- **Allons** au café ! → Let's go to the café!
- **Allez** tout droit. → Go straight ahead.

---

## 🚫 Negative Imperative

To make a command **negative**, put **ne...pas** around the verb:

| Positive | Negative |
|---------|---------|
| Parle ! | **Ne** parle **pas** ! (Don't speak!) |
| Finissons ! | **Ne** finissons **pas** ! (Let's not finish!) |
| Allez ! | **N'**allez **pas** ! (Don't go!) |
| Sois sage ! | **Ne** sois **pas** sage ! (Don't be good! — unlikely but grammatically correct) |

## 💬 Practical Examples

### Giving Instructions
- **Ouvrez** le livre à la page dix. → Open the book to page ten.
- **Lisez** la phrase. → Read the sentence.
- **Écoutez** bien. → Listen carefully.
- **Répétez** après moi. → Repeat after me.

### Making Suggestions (nous form = "Let's...")
- **Allons** au restaurant. → Let's go to the restaurant.
- **Parlons** français. → Let's speak French.
- **Finissons** nos devoirs. → Let's finish our homework.

### Polite Requests (vous form)
- **Entrez**, s'il vous plaît. → Come in, please.
- **Asseyez-vous**. → Sit down.
- **Veuillez** fermer la porte. → Please close the door. *(very formal)*

---

## 🎯 Key Rules to Remember

1. **Three forms only:** tu, nous, vous — no je, il, elle, ils forms
2. **No subject pronoun** — just the verb
3. **-ER verbs lose the -s** in the tu form: *Parle !* (not *Parles!*)
4. **-IR verbs keep the -s** in the tu form: *Finis !*
5. **Irregular verbs** (être, avoir, savoir, vouloir, aller) have their own special forms — memorise them!
