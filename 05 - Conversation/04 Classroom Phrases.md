# Classroom Phrases

These phrases are useful during lessons.

---

## What The Teacher Might Say

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Écoutez. | Listen. |
| Répétez. | Repeat. |
| Regardez. | Look. |
| Lisez. | Read. |
| Écrivez. | Write. |
| Ouvrez le livre. | Open the book. |
| Fermez le livre. | Close the book. |
| Parlez français. | Speak French. |
| Répondez à la question. | Answer the question. |

---

## What You Can Say

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Je ne comprends pas. | I do not understand. |
| Je comprends. | I understand. |
| J'ai une question. | I have a question. |
| Je ne sais pas. | I do not know. |
| Je suis prêt. | I am ready, male speaker. |
| Je suis prête. | I am ready, female speaker. |
| C'est difficile. | It is difficult. |
| C'est clair. | It is clear. |

---

## Asking For Help

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Pouvez-vous m'aider ? | Can you help me? formal |
| Tu peux m'aider ? | Can you help me? informal |
| Je peux essayer ? | Can I try? |
| Est-ce que c'est correct ? | Is this correct? |
| Quelle est la différence ? | What is the difference? |

---

## Useful Responses

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Oui. | Yes. |
| Non. | No. |
| D'accord. | Okay. |
| Peut-être. | Maybe. |
| Bien sûr. | Of course. |
| Pas de problème. | No problem. |
