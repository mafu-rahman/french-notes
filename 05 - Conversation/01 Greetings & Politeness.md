# Greetings and Politeness

These are the phrases to use immediately in real conversations.

---

## Greetings

| French | Pronunciation | English | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bonjour | boh(n)-zhoor | Hello / Good day | most situations |
| Bonsoir | boh(n)-swar | Good evening | evening |
| Salut | sah-loo | Hi / Bye | informal |
| Coucou | koo-koo | Hey | very informal |

---

## Goodbyes

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| Au revoir | oh ruh-vwar | Goodbye |
| À bientôt | ah byeh(n)-toh | See you soon |
| À demain | ah duh-meh(n) | See you tomorrow |
| À plus tard | ah ploo tar | See you later |
| À tout à l'heure | ah too tah lur | See you in a little while |
| Bonne journée | bun zhoor-nay | Have a good day |
| Bonne soirée | bun swah-ray | Have a good evening |
| Bonne nuit | bun nwee | Good night |

## Politeness

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| S'il vous plaît | seel voo pleh | Please, formal / plural |
| S'il te plaît | seel tuh pleh | Please, informal singular |
| Merci | mair-see | Thank you |
| Merci beaucoup | mair-see boh-koo | Thank you very much |
| De rien | duh ryeh(n) | You're welcome |
| Je vous en prie | zhuh voo zah(n) pree | You're welcome, formal |
| Il n'y a pas de quoi. | eel nyah pah duh kwah | It's okay / don't mention it. |
| Pardon | par-doh(n) | Sorry / excuse me |
| Désolé | day-zoh-lay | Sorry |
| Excusez-moi | eks-koo-zay mwah | Excuse me, formal / plural |
| Excuse-moi | eks-kooz mwah | Excuse me, informal singular |

---

## Asking How Someone Is

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Ça va ? | How are you? / Is it going okay? |
| Comment ça va ? | How are you? |
| Comment allez-vous ? | How are you? formal |
| Comment vas-tu ? | How are you? informal |

---

## Responding

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Ça va. | I'm okay. |
| Ça va bien. | I'm doing well. |
| Très bien, merci. | Very well, thank you. |
| Pas mal. | Not bad. |
| Comme ci, comme ça. | So-so. |
| Ça ne va pas très bien. | I'm not doing very well. |

---

## Mini Dialogue

**A:** Bonjour ! Comment ça va ?  
**B:** Ça va bien, merci. Et vous ?  
**A:** Très bien, merci.
