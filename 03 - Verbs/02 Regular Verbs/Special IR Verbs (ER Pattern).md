## ⚠️ Special -IR Verbs (Conjugate Like -ER Verbs)

Some verbs **end in -IR** but conjugate like **-ER verbs** instead of regular -IR verbs. These are exceptions — you won't see the **-iss-** stem that regular -IR verbs have.

> 🔑 **Key difference:** Regular -IR verbs add *-iss-* in nous/vous/ils forms. These special verbs do **not**.

---

## 📋 The Special Verbs

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| ouvrir | oo-vreer | to open |
| couvrir | koo-vreer | to cover |
| découvrir | day-koo-vreer | to discover |
| offrir | oh-freer | to offer |

| Verb          | Meaning                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **ouvrir**    | to open                        |
| **couvrir**   | to cover                       |
| **découvrir** | to discover                    |
| **cueillir**  | to gather / to pick            |
| **offrir**    | to offer / to give (as a gift) |


---

## 📖 Conjugation Pattern (Like -ER Verbs)

> ⚠️ **Pronunciation note:** The **-ent** ending in *ils/elles couvrir* is **completely silent**.
> Say: *couvrent* (not "couvr-ent")

### OUVRIR (to open)

| Pronoun   | Conjugation           |
| --------- | --------------------- |
| je        | j'**ouvre**           |
| tu        | tu **ouvres**         |
| il/elle   | il/elle **ouvre**     |
| nous      | nous **ouvrons**      |
| vous      | vous **ouvrez**       |
| ils/elles | ils/elles **ouvrent** |

> ✅ Notice: ends in **-e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent** — exactly like an -ER verb!

---

## 💬 Example Sentences

- J'**ouvre** la fenêtre. → I open the window.
- Il **couvre** la table. → He covers the table.
- Nous **découvrons** une nouvelle ville. → We discover a new city.
- Elle **cueille** des fleurs dans le jardin. → She picks flowers in the garden.
- Je t'**offre** ce livre. → I give you this book (as a gift).
- Vous **ouvrez** la porte, s'il vous plaît. → Please open the door.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistake

❌ Conjugating these like regular -IR verbs:
- "je ouvris" or "nous ouvrissons" → **WRONG**

✅ Correct forms use the -ER pattern:
- "j'ouvre" and "nous ouvrons" → **CORRECT**

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

Think of **ouvrir** as the "gateway" verb — it **opens** the door to this group. All these verbs share the same ER-style pattern.
