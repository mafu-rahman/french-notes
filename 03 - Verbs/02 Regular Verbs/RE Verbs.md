# -RE Verbs

Regular **-RE** verbs are less common than **-ER** verbs, but the pattern is useful.

To conjugate a regular **-RE** verb in the present tense:

1. Remove **-re**
2. Add the correct ending

Example: **vendre** -> **vend-**

---

## Present Tense Endings

| Pronoun | Ending | vendre |
|---|---|---|
| je | -s | je vends |
| tu | -s | tu vends |
| il / elle / on | no ending | il vend |
| nous | -ons | nous vendons |
| vous | -ez | vous vendez |
| ils / elles | -ent | ils vendent |

The final consonants in **vends**, **vend**, and **vendent** are usually silent.

---

## Common -RE Verbs

Pronunciations are approximate English-style guides.

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| vendre | vah(n)-dr | to sell |
| répondre | ray-poh(n)-dr | to answer / reply |
| attendre | ah-tah(n)-dr | to wait |
| entendre | ah(n)-tah(n)-dr | to hear |
| perdre | pair-dr | to lose |
| rendre | rah(n)-dr | to give back / return |
| descendre | day-sah(n)-dr | to go down |

---

## Important -RE Verb That Is Not Regular

Some useful verbs end in **-re** but do not follow the regular pattern.

| French | Pronunciation | English | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| apprendre | ah-prah(n)-dr | to learn | conjugates like prendre |

---

## Example: Répondre

| Pronoun | Conjugation |
|---|---|
| je | réponds |
| tu | réponds |
| il / elle / on | répond |
| nous | répondons |
| vous | répondez |
| ils / elles | répondent |

---

## Negative Form

- Je ne vends pas la voiture.
- Tu ne réponds pas.
- Elle n'attend pas.
- Nous ne perdons pas.

---

## Question Form

- Tu réponds ?
- Est-ce que tu réponds ?
- Réponds-tu ?

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Il vends.  
✅ Il vend.

❌ Nous répondrez.  
✅ Nous répondons.

❌ Ils attendent pronounced with "-ent."  
✅ Ils attendent = sounds like "il attend."
