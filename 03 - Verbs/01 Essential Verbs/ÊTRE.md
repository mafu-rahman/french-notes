# 🔤 ÊTRE (To Be)

**ÊTRE is THE most important French verb.** It's irregular, essential, and used everywhere. Master it completely.

---

## 📖 Meaning

**ÊTRE** = to be (to exist, to be in a state or location)

### Use ÊTRE When:
1. **Describing a characteristic or state**: Je **suis** intelligent. (I am intelligent.)
2. **Describing nationality**: Je **suis** français. (I am French.)
3. **Describing profession**: Je **suis** professeur. (I am a teacher.)
4. **Describing location**: Je **suis** à Paris. (I am in Paris.)
5. **Asking about something**: Ça **va**? (How are you?) — literally "That is going?"

---

## 📋 Present Tense Conjugation

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation | English |
|---------|------------|---|---|
| **je** | suis | **SWEE** | I am |
| **tu** | es | **AY** | You are |
| **il/elle/on** | est | **EH** | He/she/one is |
| **nous** | sommes | **SOM** | We are |
| **vous** | êtes | **EHT** | You are (formal/plural) |
| **ils/elles** | sont | **SOHN** | They are |

---

## 🎵 Pronunciation Tips

- **suis** = "swee" (NOT "su-ee")
- **es** = "eh" (short, one sound)
- **est** = "eh" (sounds like "es")
- **sommes** = "som" (like the English word "some")
- **êtes** = "eht" (sounds like "et" with a longer vowel)
- **sont** = "sohn" (nasal o + n)

## 💬 Example Sentences

### Je suis...
- Je **suis** français. (I am French.)
- Je **suis** étudiant. (I am a student.)
- Je **suis** à Paris. (I am in Paris.)
- Je **suis** happy. (I am happy.) — Note: French doesn't have "heureux" in this form

### Tu es...
- **Es**-tu français? (Are you French?)
- Tu **es** intelligent. (You are intelligent.)
- Tu **es** où? (Where are you?)

### Il/Elle est...
- **Il est** médecin. (He is a doctor.)
- **Elle est** professeur. (She is a teacher.)
- **C'est** intéressant. (It is interesting.)

### Nous sommes...
- Nous **sommes** français. (We are French.)
- Nous **sommes** à l'école. (We are at school.)
- Nous **sommes** contents. (We are happy.)

### Vous êtes...
- **Êtes**-vous français? (Are you French?) — formal
- Vous **êtes** professeur? (Are you a teacher?)

### Ils/Elles sont...
- **Ils sont** médecins. (They are doctors.)
- **Elles sont** à la maison. (They are at home.)
- **Sont**-ils français? (Are they French?)

## 🎯 Key Tips & Patterns

### ÊTRE is Highly Irregular
- You **cannot guess** the forms
- **suis, es, est, sommes, êtes, sont** share no obvious pattern
- You must **memorize** these forms

### Elision with Je
- **je suis** = "zhuh SWEE" (normal)
- **j'ai** = "zheh" (elision with avoir)
- But NO elision with ÊTRE:
  - ✅ "Je **es**...?" (no, use "tu es?")
  - ✅ "Je **suis**" (normal)

### C'est vs. Il/Elle est
Both mean "it is" but have different uses:

| Form | When to Use | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **C'est** | Before noun or adjective describing "it" | **C'est** un livre. (It's a book.) |
| **Il/Elle est** | After pronoun, with profession/description | **Il est** beau. (He is handsome.) |

- **C'est un professeur.** = It's a professor (introducing something)
- **Il est professeur.** = He is a professor (describing someone)
- **C'est intéressant.** = It's interesting (adjective about something)
- **C'est important.** = It's important (adjective about something)

### ÊTRE in Questions
Three ways to ask:

1. **Intonation** (casual): Tu **es** français? (You French?)
2. **Est-ce que** (neutral): **Est-ce que** tu **es** français?
3. **Inversion** (formal): **Es**-tu français? (formal)

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Wrong Form
❌ "Je es" → ✅ "Je **suis**"
❌ "Tu suis" → ✅ "Tu **es**"
❌ "Nous êtes" → ✅ "Nous **sommes**"

### Mistake 2: Location Without Preposition
❌ "Je suis Paris." → ✅ "Je suis **à** Paris." (add preposition!)
❌ "Je suis école." → ✅ "Je suis **à l'** école."
❌ "Je suis France." → ✅ "Je suis **en** France." (en for countries)

### Mistake 3: Missing Gender Agreement
- The pronoun stays the same, but the **adjective changes** for gender:
  - Je **suis** français. (I am French—male speaking)
  - Je **suis** française. (I am French—female speaking)

### Mistake 4: Confusing With Aller
- **ÊTRE** = permanent state (I am French)
- **ALLER** = location/motion (I am going to Paris)
- ✅ "Je **suis** à Paris" (I am in Paris—permanent/current location)
- ✅ "Je **vais** à Paris" (I am going to Paris—motion toward it)

- All reflexive verbs: Je **suis** levé. (I woke up.)
- 17 common motion verbs: Je **suis** allé. (I went.)

## 💾 ÊTRE Memorization Hack

Create a rhythm or song:
- **Je suis** (zhuh swee)
- **Tu es** (too eh)
- **Il est** (eel eh)
- **Nous sommes** (noo som)
- **Vous êtes** (voo eht)
- **Ils sont** (eel sohn)

Repeat this 10 times daily until it's automatic!
