# 🔤 ALLER (To Go)

**ALLER is the third essential French verb.** It's irregular and used for movement and future tense. Master it alongside [[ÊTRE]] and [[AVOIR]].

---

## 📖 Meaning

**ALLER** = to go (movement toward a place or into the future)

### Use ALLER When:
1. **Going somewhere**: Je **vais** à Paris. (I am going to Paris.)
2. **Immediate future**: Je **vais** manger. (I am going to eat.) — see "Futur Proche" [[Tenses]]
3. **Asking how someone is**: Ça **va**? (How are you?) / Vous **allez**? (How are you?)
4. **How something is going**: Ça **va** bien. (It's going well.)

---

## 📋 Present Tense Conjugation

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation | English |
|---------|------------|---|---|
| **je** | vais | **VEH** | I go |
| **tu** | vas | **VAH** | You go |
| **il/elle/on** | va | **VAH** | He/she/one goes |
| **nous** | allons | **AH-lohn** | We go |
| **vous** | allez | **AH-lay** | You go (formal/plural) |
| **ils/elles** | vont | **VOHN** (nasal) | They go |

---

## 🎵 Pronunciation Tips

- **vais** = "veh" (sounds like "ve" or "va")
- **vas** = "vah" (short vowel)
- **va** = "vah" (short, one sound)
- **allons** = "ah-lohn" (nasal ending)
- **allez** = "ah-lay"
- **vont** = "vohn" (nasal o)

## 💬 Example Sentences

### Je vais...
- Je **vais** à Paris. (I am going to Paris.)
- Je **vais** à l'école. (I am going to school.)
- Je **vais** manger. (I am going to eat.) — immediate future
- Je **vais** bien. (I'm doing well.)

### Tu vas...
- Tu **vas** où? (Where are you going?)
- Où **vas**-tu? (Where are you going?) — formal question
- Tu **vas** bien? (Are you doing well?)

### Il/Elle va...
- **Il va** au café. (He is going to the café.)
- **Elle va** en France. (She is going to France.)
- **Ça va**? (How are things?) / **Ça va** bien! (Things are going well!)

### Nous allons...
- Nous **allons** au parc. (We are going to the park.)
- Nous **allons** être en retard. (We are going to be late.)
- Nous **allons** bien. (We are doing well.)

### Vous allez...
- Vous **allez** où? (Where are you going?) — formal
- Vous **allez** bien? (Are you doing well?) — formal/plural

### Ils/Elles vont...
- **Ils vont** à l'école. (They are going to school.)
- **Elles vont** en vacances. (They are going on vacation.)
- **Vont**-ils bien? (Are they doing well?)

---

## 🎯 Key Tips & Patterns

### ALLER is Highly Irregular
- Forms: **vais, vas, va, allons, allez, vont**
- Comes from Latin "vadere" (not "aller")
- Must memorize completely

### Immediate Future: ALLER + Infinitive
**ALLER + infinitive verb** = "going to do something"

- Je **vais** manger. (I'm going to eat.) NOT "I go eating"
- Tu **vas** partir. (You're going to leave.)
- Il **va** arriver. (He's going to arrive.)
- Nous **allons** danser. (We're going to dance.)
- Vous **allez** comprendre. (You're going to understand.)
- Ils **vont** finir. (They're going to finish.)

This is very common in spoken French for talking about the near future.

### À vs. En vs. No Preposition

| Usage | Phrase | Translation |
|-------|--------|---|
| City | Je **vais à** Paris | I'm going to Paris |
| Country (feminine) | Je **vais en** France | I'm going to France |
| Country (masculine) | Je **vais au** Japon | I'm going to Japan |
| Place/Building | Je **vais à** l'école | I'm going to school |
| No destination | Je **vais**. | I'm going. |

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Wrong Form
❌ "Je vais" vs "Tu vais" → ✅ "Tu **vas**" (not vais)
❌ "Nous allons" vs "Vous allons" → ✅ "Vous **allez**" (not allons)
❌ "Ils vont" vs "Ils allons" → ✅ "Ils **vont**" (not allons)

### Mistake 2: Forgetting the Infinitive in Immediate Future
❌ "Je vais manger" as "I go to eat" (correct meaning, but different structure)
✅ "Je vais manger" = "I'm going to eat" (this IS the structure)

### Mistake 3: Using ALLER Instead of ÊTRE for States
❌ "Je vais français" → ✅ "Je **suis** français"
❌ "Je vais heureux" → ✅ "Je **suis** heureux"

Use **ÊTRE** for permanent states, **ALLER** for motion or immediate future.

### Mistake 4: Wrong Preposition
❌ "Je vais en Paris" → ✅ "Je **vais à** Paris"
❌ "Je vais à France" → ✅ "Je **vais en** France"

## 📚 Passé Composé Connection

**ALLER** uses **ÊTRE** (not AVOIR) as auxiliary in passé composé:

- Je **suis** allé. (I went.)
- Tu **es** allée. (You went—feminine)
- Il **est** allé. (He went.)
- Elle **est** allée. (She went.)
- Nous **sommes** allés. (We went.)
- Vous **êtes** allés. (You went.)
- Ils **sont** allés. (They went.)
- Elles **sont** allées. (They went—all feminine.)

The past participle **allé** changes with gender/number. See [[Passé Composé]] for details.
