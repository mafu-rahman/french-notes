# 🔤 AVOIR (To Have)

**AVOIR is the second most important French verb.** Master it alongside [[ÊTRE]]. It's irregular and used constantly.

---

## 📖 Meaning

**AVOIR** = to have (to possess)

### Use AVOIR When:
1. **Possessing something**: J'**ai** un chat. (I have a cat.)
2. **Expressing age**: J'**ai** 20 ans. (I am 20 years old.) — literally "I have 20 years"
3. **Expressing hunger/thirst/cold**: J'**ai** faim. (I am hungry.) — literally "I have hunger"
4. **Needing something**: J'**ai** besoin d'aide. (I need help.) — literally "I have need of help"
5. **As auxiliary in passé composé**: J'**ai** mangé. (I ate.) — see [[Passé Composé]]

---

## 📋 Present Tense Conjugation

| Pronoun        | Conjugation | Pronunciation    | English                  |
| -------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| **je**         | ai          | **AY** or **EH** | I have                   |
| **tu**         | as          | **AH**           | You have                 |
| **il/elle/on** | a           | **AH**           | He/she/one has           |
| **nous**       | avons       | **AH-vohn**      | We have                  |
| **vous**       | avez        | **AH-vay**       | You have (formal/plural) |
| **ils/elles**  | ont         | **OHN** (nasal)  | They have                |

---

## 🎵 Pronunciation Tips

- **ai** = "ay" or "eh" (short vowel sound)
- **as** = "ah" (short)
- **a** = "ah" (short, one sound)
- **avons** = "ah-vohn" (nasal ending)
- **avez** = "ah-vay"
- **ont** = "ohn" (nasal o)

## 🚫 Negative Form

Use **ne...pas** around the conjugated form of **avoir**.

| Pronoun | Negative         | English           |
| ------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| je      | je n'ai pas      | I do not have     |
| tu      | tu n'as pas      | you do not have   |
| il      | il n'a pas       | he does not have  |
| elle    | elle n'a pas     | she does not have |
| nous    | nous n'avons pas | we do not have    |
| vous    | vous n'avez pas  | you do not have   |
| ils     | ils n'ont pas    | they do not have  |
| elles   | elles n'ont pas  | they do not have  |

Examples:

- Je n'ai pas de livre.
- Nous n'avons pas de voiture.
- Ils n'ont pas d'amis.

## ❓ Interrogative Form

With inversion, place the verb before the subject pronoun.

| Statement | Question | English |
|---|---|---|
| j'ai | ai-je ? | do I have? |
| tu as | as-tu ? | do you have? |
| il a | a-t-il ? | does he have? |
| elle a | a-t-elle ? | does she have? |
| nous avons | avons-nous ? | do we have? |
| vous avez | avez-vous ? | do you have? |
| ils ont | ont-ils ? | do they have? |
| elles ont | ont-elles ? | do they have? |

Use **-t-** in **a-t-il** and **a-t-elle** to make pronunciation smoother.

## 💬 Example Sentences

### J'ai...
- J'**ai** un chat. (I have a cat.)
- J'**ai** un livre. (I have a book.)
- J'**ai** faim. (I am hungry.)
- J'**ai** 20 ans. (I am 20 years old.)
- J'**ai** besoin d'aide. (I need help.)

### Tu as...
- Tu **as** un stylo? (Do you have a pen?)
- Tu **as** froid? (Are you cold?)
- Tu **as** sommeil? (Are you sleepy?)

### Il/Elle a...
- **Il a** un ordinateur. (He has a computer.)
- **Elle a** des livres. (She has some books.)
- **On a** faim. (We are hungry.)

### Nous avons...
- Nous **avons** une maison. (We have a house.)
- Nous **avons** des amis. (We have friends.)
- Nous **avons** chaud. (We are hot.)

### Vous avez...
- Vous **avez** un crayon? (Do you have a pencil?)
- Vous **avez** besoin de quelque chose? (Do you need something?)

### Ils/Elles ont...
- **Ils ont** un chat. (They have a cat.)
- **Elles ont** des robes. (They have dresses.)
- **Ont**-ils faim? (Are they hungry?)

## 🎯 Key Tips & Patterns

### AVOIR is Highly Irregular
- Forms: **ai, as, a, avons, avez, ont**
- NO pattern—must memorize
- Completely different from expected conjugation

### Expressions With AVOIR

Unlike English, French uses AVOIR for many states where English uses "to be":

| French | English | Literal |
|--------|---------|---------|
| J'**ai** faim | I am hungry | I have hunger |
| J'**ai** soif | I am thirsty | I have thirst |
| J'**ai** froid | I am cold | I have cold |
| J'**ai** chaud | I am hot | I have heat |
| J'**ai** sommeil | I am sleepy | I have sleep |
| J'**ai** peur | I am afraid | I have fear |
| J'**ai** raison | I am right | I have reason |
| J'**ai** tort | I am wrong | I have wrong/fault |
| J'**ai** besoin | I need | I have need |
| J'**ai** envie | I want/feel like | I have desire |
| J'**ai** honte | I am ashamed | I have shame |
| J'**ai** de la chance | I am lucky | I have luck |

### Elision With j'ai
- **je ai** → **j'**ai (elision occurs)
- **J'ai faim.** (I am hungry.)
- NOT "Je ai" — always "J'ai"

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Wrong Form
❌ "Je as" → ✅ "Je **ai**"
❌ "Tu ai" → ✅ "Tu **as**"
❌ "Nous ont" → ✅ "Nous **avons**"

### Mistake 2: Using AVOIR Instead of ÊTRE
❌ "J'ai 20 ans" — Actually CORRECT! (French says "I have 20 years")
❌ "J'ai faim" — Actually CORRECT! (French says "I have hunger")

This is a French quirk—use AVOIR for these expressions, not ÊTRE!

### Mistake 3: Forgetting Elision
❌ "Je ai un livre" → ✅ "**J'ai** un livre"

### Mistake 4: Confusing AVOIR/ÊTRE in Passé Composé
Most verbs use **AVOIR**: J'**ai** mangé. (I ate.)
Some verbs use **ÊTRE**: Je **suis** allé. (I went.)
See [[Passé Composé]] for the complete list.

## 📚 Passé Composé Connection

**AVOIR** is the auxiliary verb for most verbs in the past:

- J'**ai** mangé. (I ate.)
- Tu **as** bu. (You drank.)
- Il **a** parlé. (He spoke.)
- Nous **avons** fini. (We finished.)
- Vous **avez** marché. (You walked.)
- Ils **ont** compris. (They understood.)

See [[Passé Composé]] for full details.

- **Nous avons** (noo ah-vohn)

- **Ils ont** (eel ohn)

Repeat 10 times daily!

## ✅ Quick Practice

Conjugate AVOIR for each situation:

1. I have a cat: J'___ un chat → **ai**
2. You (tu) have a book: Tu ___ un livre → **as**
3. She has a pencil: Elle ___ un crayon → **a**
4. We have friends: Nous ___ des amis → **avons**
5. You (formal) have a house: Vous ___ une maison → **avez**
6. They have a car: Ils ___ une voiture → **ont**
7. I am hungry: J'___ faim → **ai**
8. Are they cold?: Ont-ils ___? → **froid**
