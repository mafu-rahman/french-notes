Learn French colors for describing objects, people, and in conversation.

## 🎯 Key Tips

### Colors Are Adjectives
Colors are adjectives in French, so they:
1. **Come after the noun** (NOT before, like in English)
   - English: "the red car"
   - French: "la voiture rouge" (the car red)

2. **Agree with gender and number** (mostly)
   - Masculine: un livre bleu (a blue book)
   - Feminine: une maison bleue (a blue house)
   - Plural: des livres bleus (blue books)

### Gender Agreement

Most colors add **-e** for feminine:
- **bleu** → **bleue** (blue)
- **vert** → **verte** (green)
- **gris** → **grise** (gray)

Some colors are **invariable** (don't change):
- **orange** (orange) - stays "orange" for all genders/numbers
- **rose** (pink) - becomes "rose" for all... wait, actually "rose" does change sometimes
- **brun** (brown) → **brune** (feminine)

### Number Agreement

Most colors add **-s** for plural:
- **des chats noirs** (black cats)
- **des maisons blanches** (white houses)
- **des voitures grises** (gray cars)

**Invariable colors** (don't add -s):
- **orange** - un stylo orange, des stylos orange
- Sometimes compound colors don't agree

## 💬 Examples

### Singular
- **un livre bleu** (a blue book)
- **une robe rouge** (a red dress)
- **un chat noir** (a black cat)
- **une maison blanche** (a white house)

### Plural
- **des livres bleus** (blue books)
- **des robes rouges** (red dresses)
- **des chats noirs** (black cats)
- **des maisons blanches** (white houses)

### In Sentences
- Le **ciel est bleu.** (The sky is blue.)
- La **voiture est verte.** (The car is green.)
- Les **fleurs sont jaunes.** (The flowers are yellow.)
- Mes **yeux sont bruns.** (My eyes are brown.)

---

## 🎨 Shades & Variations

### Light & Dark
- **bleu clair** = light blue
- **bleu foncé** = dark blue
- **bleu marine** = navy blue

### Compound Colors
- **bleu-vert** = blue-green
- **jaune-orange** = yellow-orange

**Note:** Compound colors typically stay the same (don't agree):
- **des robes bleu-vert** (blue-green dresses) — no extra -s or -e usually

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Color Before the Noun
❌ "une rouge robe" → ✅ "une **robe rouge**" (color comes AFTER)

### Mistake 2: Forgetting Gender Agreement
❌ "une voiture bleu" → ✅ "une voiture **bleue**" (feminine -e)
❌ "une maison vert" → ✅ "une maison **verte**"

### Mistake 3: Forgetting Number Agreement
❌ "des chats noir" → ✅ "des chats **noirs**" (plural -s)
❌ "des robes rouge" → ✅ "des robes **rouges**"

### Mistake 4: Changing Invariable Colors
❌ "un stylo oranges" → ✅ "un stylo **orange**"
❌ "des stylos oranges" → ✅ "des stylos **orange**" (no -s for compound colors)

### Mistake 5: Using English Word Order
❌ "black cat" in French is NOT "black chat" → ✅ "**chat noir**"
