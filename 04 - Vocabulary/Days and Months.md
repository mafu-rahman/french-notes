# Days and Months

Use this note for basic calendar vocabulary: days of the week, months, seasons, and simple date phrases.

Pronunciations are approximate English-style guides.

---

## Days of the Week

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| lundi | luh(n)-dee | Monday |
| mardi | mar-dee | Tuesday |
| mercredi | mair-kruh-dee | Wednesday |
| jeudi | zhuh-dee | Thursday |
| vendredi | vah(n)-druh-dee | Friday |
| samedi | sam-dee | Saturday |
| dimanche | dee-mah(n)sh | Sunday |

In French, days are usually written with lowercase letters.

Examples:

- lundi
- mardi
- dimanche

## Asking the Date

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| Quelle est la date ? | kel eh lah dat | What is the date? |
| Nous sommes quel jour ? | noo som kel zhoor | What day is it? |
| C'est lundi. | say luh(n)-dee | It is Monday. |
| Nous sommes le 5 juin. | noo som luh sank zhweh(n) | It is June 5. |

---

## Saying Dates

French dates usually use:

**le + number + month**

Examples:

- le 5 juin = June 5
- le 14 juillet = July 14
- le 25 décembre = December 25

For the first day of the month, use **premier**:

- le premier janvier = January 1
- le premier mai = May 1

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Lundi with a capital letter in the middle of a sentence.  
✅ lundi

❌ Janvier with a capital letter in the middle of a sentence.  
✅ janvier

❌ le 1 janvier  
✅ le premier janvier
