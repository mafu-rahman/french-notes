# Directions and Location

Use this note for directions, position words, and simple movement instructions.

Pronunciations are approximate English-style guides.

---

## Direction Words

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| à droite | ah drwat | to the right |
| à gauche | ah gosh | to the left |
| tout droit | too drwah | straight ahead |
| en face | ah(n) fahss | opposite / facing |
| derrière | dair-ryair | behind |
| devant | duh-vah(n) | in front of |
| entre | ah(n)-truh | between |
| ici | ee-see | here |
| là | lah | there |
