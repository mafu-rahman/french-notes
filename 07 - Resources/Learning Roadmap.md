# 📚 French Learning Goals: A1 → B2 Proficiency Roadmap

Track your progress through beginner (A1) → elementary (A2) → intermediate (B1) → upper-intermediate (B2) levels.

## 🎯 A2 Level (Elementary) — Next Steps

**Goal**: Handle everyday situations; describe past events; make simple plans; read simple texts.

### 📚 Grammar (A2)
- [ ] [[Passé Composé]] (Past Tense) — Know AVOIR vs. ÊTRE, past participles
- [ ] [[Imperative Mood]] (Commands) — tu form, vous form, nous form
- [ ] [[Object Pronouns]] — Direct (le, la, les) and indirect (lui, leur)
- [ ] [[Reflexive Verbs]] — se lever, s'appeler, se coucher
- [ ] [[Adjectives and Agreement]] — Gender, number, position (BAGS rule)
- [ ] [[Adverbs]] — Formation and placement
- [ ] [[Comparative and Superlative]] — plus/moins/aussi...que, le plus, le moins

### 🔤 More Verbs (A2)
- [ ] [[FAIRE]] (to make/do) — Present tense
- [ ] [[POUVOIR]] (can/to be able) — Modal verb
- [ ] [[VOULOIR]] (to want) — Modal verb
- [ ] [[DEVOIR]] (must/have to) — Modal verb
- [ ] More -ER/-IR/-RE verbs in passé composé

### 📖 Vocabulary (A2)
- [ ] Food & Drink (food vocabulary, restaurant phrases)
- [ ] Clothing & Fashion
- [ ] Body Parts & Health
- [ ] House & Furniture
- [ ] Travel & Transportation
- [ ] Hobbies & Leisure Activities

### 💬 Conversation (A2)
- [ ] Describe past events (what you did yesterday, last week)
- [ ] Order food at a restaurant
- [ ] Ask for directions
- [ ] Talk about plans (Je vais + infinitive)
- [ ] Describe people and objects

### 🗣️ Speaking & Writing Skills (A2)
- [ ] Write short messages/emails
- [ ] Understand basic texts (menus, signs, simple articles)
- [ ] Participate in simple conversations

## 🎓 Study Tips by Level

### A1 Focus
- Drill the three essential verbs DAILY
- Memorize vocabulary with articles (un/une)
- Practice pronunciation consistently
- Focus on fluency over accuracy

### A2 Focus
- Master passé composé before moving forward
- Learn irregular verbs in context
- Start reading simple French texts
- Practice speaking in past tense

### B1+ Focus
- Read French literature and news
- Watch French movies with subtitles
- Engage in language exchange
- Focus on nuance and natural expression
