## 🔤 Accents Quick Reference

| Mark | Name | Example | Main Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| é | accent aigu | café | closed "ay" sound |
| è | accent grave | mère | open "eh" sound |
| ê | accent circonflexe | être | spelling/pronunciation marker |
| ë | tréma | Noël | separate vowel sounds |
| ç | cédille | français | makes c sound like s |
| à / où | accent grave | à, où | distinguishes words |

Key pairs:

- **a** = has, **à** = to / at
- **ou** = or, **où** = where
- **sur** = on, **sûr** = sure

## 🔤 Subject Pronouns & ÊTRE/AVOIR

### Subject Pronouns
| Pronoun | Meaning | Tu vs. Vous |
|---------|---------|---|
| je | I | — |
| tu | You (singular) | Friends, family, peers |
| il/elle | He/She | — |
| on | We (informal) | Often used instead of nous |
| nous | We (formal) | Group, formal register |
| vous | You (plural/formal) | Strangers, elders, groups |
| ils/elles | They | Masculine or mixed / All feminine |

### ÊTRE Conjugation
| Pronoun | Form | Sound |
|---------|------|-------|
| je | **suis** | swee |
| tu | **es** | eh |
| il/elle/on | **est** | eh |
| nous | **sommes** | som |
| vous | **êtes** | eht |
| ils/elles | **sont** | sohn |

### AVOIR Conjugation
| Pronoun | Form | Sound |
|---------|------|-------|
| je | **ai** | eh/ay |
| tu | **as** | ah |
| il/elle/on | **a** | ah |
| nous | **avons** | ah-vohn |
| vous | **avez** | ah-vay |
| ils/elles | **ont** | ohn |

### ALLER Conjugation
| Pronoun | Form | Sound |
|---------|------|-------|
| je | **vais** | veh |
| tu | **vas** | vah |
| il/elle/on | **va** | vah |
| nous | **allons** | ah-lohn |
| vous | **allez** | ah-lay |
| ils/elles | **vont** | vohn |

## 📚 Articles at a Glance

### Definite Articles (The)
| Gender | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Masculine | **le** | **les** |
| Feminine | **la** | **les** |
| Vowel/Silent H | **l'** | **les** |

### Indefinite Articles (A/An/Some)
| Gender | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Masculine | **un** | **des** |
| Feminine | **une** | **des** |

### Contracted Articles
| Preposition + Article | Form |
|---|---|
| à + le | **au** |
| à + les | **aux** |
| de + le | **du** |
| de + les | **des** |
| à + la | à la (no contraction) |
| de + la | de la (no contraction) |

## 🔢 Numbers Cheat Sheet

### 0-20
zéro, un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq, six, sept, huit, neuf, dix, onze, douze, treize, quatorze, quinze, seize, dix-sept, dix-huit, dix-neuf

### Tens
20 - vingt
30 - trente
40 - quarante
50 - cinquante
60 - soixante
70 - soixante-dix
80 - quatre-vingts
90 - quatre-vingt-dix
100 - cent
1000 - mille
1,000,000 - un million

### Special Rules
- **vingt** & **cents** get -s when multiplied AND not followed by other numbers
  - 200 = deux cents (no -s before another number)
  - 210 = deux cent dix (no -s)
- 70-90 use old counting (pay attention!)

## 🗣️ Questions Quick Guide

### Three Ways to Ask

**Intonation** (casual)  
→ Tu aimes le café?

**Est-ce que** (neutral)  
→ Est-ce que tu aimes le café?

**Inversion** (formal)  
→ Aimes-tu le café?

### Question Words
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Où? | Where? | Où vas-tu? |
| Quand? | When? | Quand arrives-tu? |
| Comment? | How? | Comment ça va? |
| Pourquoi? | Why? | Pourquoi tu ris? |
| Qui? | Who? | Qui est-ce? |
| Quoi? | What? | De quoi parles-tu? |
| Quel? | Which? | Quel jour? |

## 💬 Essential Phrases

### Politeness
- **S'il vous plaît** = Please (formal)
- **Merci** = Thank you
- **De rien** = You're welcome
- **Excusez-moi** = Excuse me
- **Pardon** = Sorry

### Greetings
- **Bonjour** = Hello/Good day
- **Bonsoir** = Good evening
- **Au revoir** = Goodbye

### Introduction
- **Je m'appelle...** = My name is...
- **Enchanté** = Pleased to meet you
- **Ça va?** = How are you?
- **Ça va bien** = I'm fine

### Useful
- **Je ne comprends pas** = I don't understand
- **Parlez plus lentement** = Speak more slowly
- **Pouvez-vous m'aider?** = Can you help me?
