# 🔗 Possession with DE (of/from)

In French, there is **no apostrophe-s** like in English. Instead, ownership is expressed using **de** (of).

> ✅ English: *the boy's pencil*
> ✅ French: *le crayon **du** garçon* (the pencil **of the** boy)

---

## 📋 Two Key Situations

### 1. Before Proper Nouns (names of people/places)

When **de** is followed directly by a **proper noun** (a name), there is **no contraction** — just use **de** + the name.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| C'est le livre **de Marie**. | It's Marie's book. |
| Ils arrivent **de Paris**. | They are arriving from Paris. |

---

### 2. Before Definite Articles + Common Nouns

When **de** is followed by **le / la / l' / les**, it may **contract**. This is essential to get right!

| de + | Becomes | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **le** | **du** | le bureau **du** directeur *(the director's office)* |
| **la** | **de la** | la salle **de la** secrétaire *(the secretary's room)* |
| **l'** | **de l'** | le crayon **de l'**étudiant *(the student's pencil)* |
| **les** | **des** | les cahiers **des** étudiants *(the students' notebooks)* |

> See [[Contracted Articles]] for the full contraction rules.

---

## 📝 Examples from Class

**Before proper nouns:**
- C'est le livre **de Marie**. → It's Marie's book.
- Ils arrivent **de Paris**. → They are arriving from Paris.

**Before definite articles (masculine singular → du):**
- Paris est la capitale **de la** France. → Paris is the capital of France.
- Où est le bureau **du** directeur? → Where is the director's office?
- Il rentre **du** cinéma. → He is returning from the cinema.
- Voilà le chapeau **du** garçon. → There is the boy's cap.

**Before definite articles (with l'):**
- Voilà le livre **de l'**étudiant. → There is the student's book.
- Où est le crayon **de l'**étudiant? → Where is the student's pencil?

**Before definite articles (feminine singular → de la):**
- Je n'aime pas la robe **de la** fille. → I don't like the girl's dress.

**Before definite articles (plural → des):**
- Où est la salle **des** professeurs? → Where is the teachers' room?
- Les cahiers **des** étudiants sont sur la table. → The students' notebooks are on the table.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Using apostrophe-s (English habit)
❌ "le livre de's Marie" → ✅ **le livre de Marie**

### Mistake 2: Forgetting to contract with le/les
❌ "le bureau de le directeur" → ✅ **le bureau du directeur**
❌ "les cahiers de les étudiants" → ✅ **les cahiers des étudiants**

### Mistake 3: Contracting with la or l'
❌ "la robe du la fille" → ✅ **la robe de la fille**

---

## 🎯 Quick Summary

| After de | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Proper noun (Marie, Paris...) | de + name, no change |
| le | → **du** |
| la | → **de la** (no change) |
| l' | → **de l'** (no change) |
| les | → **des** |
