# 📚 Grammar Tips & Rules

Supplementary reference for grammar patterns, common issues, and exceptions.

---

## 🧱 Basic Sentence Order

The basic French sentence pattern is:

**subject + verb + object**

| Part | French | English |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Je | I |
| Verb | mange | eat |
| Object | une pomme | an apple |

Example:

- Je mange une pomme. = I eat an apple.
- Tu as un stylo. = You have a pen.
- Elle lit un livre. = She reads a book.

Negation usually wraps around the verb:

- Je ne mange pas de pomme. = I do not eat an apple / any apple.

## 🎯 Gender & Number

### Every Noun Has Gender
- **Masculine**: le, un, ce → He/It (il)
- **Feminine**: la, une, cette → She/It (elle)
- Gender is **arbitrary** — not always logical
- **Always learn nouns with their articles**

### Plural Additions
- Most nouns add **-s**: un chat → des chats
- Words ending in **-au, -eau, -eu** add **-x**: un bureau → des bureaux
- Most words ending in **-ou** add **-s**, but 7 common exceptions add **-x**: un bijou → des bijoux
- Words ending in **-al** change to **-aux**: un journal → des journaux (with some exceptions)
- Most words ending in **-ail** add **-s**, but some change to **-aux**: un travail → des travaux
- In compounds like **arc-en-ciel**, only the first noun becomes plural: des arcs-en-ciel

See [[Formation of Plurals]] for the full beginner guide.

## 💬 Subject Pronouns Reminder

| Pronoun | Use When |
|---------|----------|
| je | First person singular |
| tu | Friend, family, peer, child |
| il | Masculine singular noun |
| elle | Feminine singular noun |
| on | Informal "we" / Impersonal "one" |
| nous | Formal "we" / Plural person |
| vous | Formal singular you / Plural you |
| ils | Masculine or mixed plural group |
| elles | All-feminine plural group |

**Key**: Tu vs. Vous depends on RELATIONSHIP, not number!

## 🔤 Verb Conjugation Basics

### Three Regular Verb Types

#### -ER Verbs (Most Common)
Example: **parler** (to speak)
- je parle
- tu parles
- il/elle parle
- nous parlons
- vous parlez
- ils/elles parlent

#### -IR Verbs
Example: **finir** (to finish)
- je finis
- tu finis
- il/elle finit
- nous finissons
- vous finissez
- ils/elles finissent

#### -RE Verbs
Example: **vendre** (to sell)
- je vends
- tu vends
- il/elle vend
- nous vendons
- vous vendez
- ils/elles vendent

---

## 📚 The Three Most Important Verbs

### ÊTRE (To Be)
Use for: States, characteristics, nationality, location, profession
- je suis, tu es, il est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils sont

### AVOIR (To Have)
Use for: Possession, age, hunger/thirst/cold, as auxiliary in passé composé
- j'ai, tu as, il a, nous avons, vous avez, ils ont

### ALLER (To Go)
Use for: Motion, immediate future (aller + infinitive)
- je vais, tu vas, il va, nous allons, vous allez, ils vont

## 🗣️ Questions

### Three Question Patterns

**1. Intonation (Casual)**
- Statement with rising tone at the end
- "Tu aimes le café?" (with upward inflection)

**2. Est-ce que... (Neutral)**
- "Est-ce que" + statement
- "Est-ce que tu aimes le café?"

**3. Inversion (Formal)**
- Reverse subject pronoun and verb
- "Aimes-tu le café?" (with hyphen)
- Add -t- if needed: "Aime-t-il le café?"

## ✍️ Adjectives

### Position
- **Before noun**: Some common beauty, age, goodness, and size adjectives (BAGS)
  - une belle maison (a beautiful house)
  - un jeune homme (a young man)
  - un bon livre (a good book)

- **After noun**: Most other adjectives, including colors, nationality, and material
  - un homme français (a French man)
  - une robe rouge (a red dress)
  - une table ronde (a round table)

### Gender & Number Agreement
- Add **-e** for feminine: bleu → bleue
- Add **-s** for plural: bleu → bleus
- Add **-es** for feminine plural: bleu → bleues
- Some are invariable (orange, rose, etc.)

### Comparison
- **plus...que** = more...than
  - Il est plus grand que moi. (He is taller than me.)
- **moins...que** = less...than
  - Je suis moins intelligente que lui. (I am less intelligent than him.)
- **aussi...que** = as...as
  - Tu es aussi rapide que moi. (You are as fast as me.)

## 🔤 Adverbs

### Formation (Usually)
- Often take the feminine adjective form + **-ment**
- lent / lente → lentement (slowly)
- rapide → rapidement (quickly)
- heureux / heureuse → heureusement (fortunately)

### Common Adverbs
- **très** = very
- **assez** = enough/quite
- **trop** = too much
- **peu** = little
- **beaucoup** = a lot
- **bien** = well
- **mal** = badly
- **aussi** = also
- **ne...pas** = not
- **ne...jamais** = never
- **ne...rien** = nothing
- **ne...aucun** = none

---

## 🚫 Negation

### Basic Negative
**ne...pas** around the verb
- Je **ne** suis **pas** français. (I am not French.)
- Elle **ne** va **pas** bien. (She is not doing well.)

### In Passé Composé
**ne** before auxiliary, **pas** after
- Je **ne** suis **pas** allé. (I did not go.)
- Elle **n'a** pas mangé. (She did not eat.)

### Other Negatives
- **ne...jamais** = never (Je ne suis jamais allé)
- **ne...rien** = nothing (Je ne vois rien)
- **ne...aucun** = none (Je n'ai aucun livre)
- **ne...plus** = no longer (Je ne vais plus)
- **ne...ni...ni** = neither...nor (Je n'aime ni le café ni le thé)

## 📝 Common Phrases

### Polite
- **S'il vous plaît** = Please (formal)
- **S'il te plaît** = Please (informal)
- **Merci** = Thank you
- **De rien** = You're welcome
- **Excusez-moi** = Excuse me
- **Pardon** = Sorry

### Greetings
- **Bonjour** = Hello/Good day
- **Bonsoir** = Good evening
- **Bonne nuit** = Good night
- **Au revoir** = Goodbye
- **À bientôt** = See you soon
- **Salut** = Hi/Bye (informal)

### Essentials
- **Ça va?** = How are you? (casual)
- **Ça va bien** = I'm doing well
- **Je m'appelle...** = My name is...
- **Enchanté** = Pleased to meet you
- **Parlez plus lentement, s'il vous plaît** = Speak more slowly, please

---

## 🎯 Order of Adjectives & Adverbs

In complex sentences:
- Adverbs typically come after the verb: Je parle **bien** français
- But they can come at the start for emphasis: **Heureusement**, il va bien
- Multiple adjectives: **un petit livre blanc** (size + color)
- Adverbs of quantity before: **très beau**, **assez intelligent**
