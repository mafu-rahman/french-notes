# ♀️ Feminine of Nouns and Adjectives

In French, most nouns and adjectives have a **masculine** and a **feminine** form. The feminine is usually built from the masculine by changing or adding to the ending. Below are the main rules with examples from class.

---

## 📋 The Rules

### Rule 1 — Add **-e** to the masculine form

The most common rule: just add a silent **-e**.

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| ami | ami**e** | friend |
| petit | petit**e** | small |
| sûr | sûr**e** | sure |
| lourd | lourd**e** | heavy |

> 💡 Adding **-e** often makes a silent final consonant **pronounced**: *petit* (silent t) → *petite* (you hear the "t").

---

### Rule 2 — Words ending in **-e** do not change

If the masculine already ends in **-e**, the feminine is identical.

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| facile | facile | easy |
| le concierge | la concierge | caretaker |

---

### Rule 3 — Words ending in **-er** → **-ère**

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| l'étranger | l'étrang**ère** | foreigner |
| cher | ch**ère** | dear / expensive |

---

### Rule 4 — Words ending in **-f** → **-ve**

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| vif | vi**ve** | lively |
| actif | acti**ve** | active |
| sportif | sporti**ve** | sporty |
| neuf | neu**ve** | new |

---

### Rule 5 — Words ending in **-x** → **-se**

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| heureux | heureu**se** | happy |
| ambitieux | ambitieu**se** | ambitious |
| dangereux | dangereu**se** | dangerous |

> ⚠️ **Exceptions:**
> - doux → **douce** (soft)
> - faux → **fausse** (false)
> - roux → **rousse** (red-haired)
> - vieux → **vieille** (old)

---

### Rule 6 — Adjectives ending in **-c** → **-che** or **-que**

| Masculine | Feminine    | Meaning |
| --------- | ----------- | ------- |
| blanc     | blan**che** | white   |
| sec       | sè**che**   | dry     |
| turc      | tur**que**  | Turkish |
| grec      | grec**que** | Greek   |

---

### Rule 7 — Words ending in **-eur / -teur**

Most agent nouns and adjectives ending in **-eur** become **-euse**; many in **-teur** become **-trice**.

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| menteur | ment**euse** | liar |
| travailleur | travaill**euse** | hard-working |
| chanteur | chant**euse** | singer |
| acteur | ac**trice** | actor / actress |
| instituteur | institu**trice** | teacher |
| directeur | direc**trice** | director |
| vengeur | vengeresse | avenger |
| empereur | impératrice | emperor / empress |

> 💡 Rough guide: if the word comes from a verb you can hear (*chanter → chanteur/chanteuse*), it usually takes **-euse**. More formal/Latin-based **-teur** words take **-trice**.

---

### Rule 8 — Doubling the final consonant + **-e**

Words ending in **-el, -eil, -en, -on, -et, -s** often **double the final consonant** before adding **-e**.

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| naturel | naturel**le** | natural |
| pareil | pareil**le** | similar |
| ancien | ancien**ne** | old / former |
| bon | bon**ne** | good |
| gentil | gentil**le** | kind |
| gros | gros**se** | big / fat |
| bas | bas**se** | low |

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting the **-e** when describing something feminine → *la maison est petit* → ✅ *la maison est petit**e***.
- ❌ Treating every **-x** word the same → remember the exceptions (*douce, fausse, rousse, vieille*).
- ❌ Not doubling the consonant → *bonne*, not *bone*; *ancienne*, not *anciene*.

## 🎯 Quick Summary

| Ending | Becomes | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| consonant | + **e** | petit → petite |
| -e | no change | facile → facile |
| -er | -ère | cher → chère |
| -f | -ve | actif → active |
| -x | -se | heureux → heureuse |
| -c | -che / -que | blanc → blanche |
| -eur / -teur | -euse / -trice | chanteur → chanteuse |
| -el, -on, -en... | double + e | bon → bonne |
